Turn any video or GIF from a tweet into animated WhatsApp stickers, in seconds and entirely on your iPhone.

HOW IT WORKS
• Paste a tweet link, or a direct link to a video or GIF
• Or share a tweet to Stickerer from the share sheet in X: the app opens and starts converting right away
• Tap "Add to WhatsApp" and your pack is ready to send

MADE FOR STICKERS
• Animated 512×512 stickers that fit WhatsApp's size limit automatically
• Fit the whole clip with padding, or fill the frame and crop
• Split a longer clip into 3 stickers
• Transparent, die-cut sticker look in the app

YOUR STICKER LIBRARY
• Every sticker you make is saved on your device
• Tap a sticker in your library to add it to a new pack
• Long-press to remove stickers you no longer want

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Videos are converted on your device
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Open source: github.com/broemp/stickerer
