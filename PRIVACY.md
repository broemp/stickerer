# Privacy Policy – Stickerer

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Stickerer does not have accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer does not collect, store or receive any data about you.

## What the app does with your data
- **Links you paste or share.** To find the video in a tweet, the app sends the tweet's ID to the public tweet lookup service `api.fxtwitter.com`, then downloads the video from the address that service returns. That service sees the request (including your IP address) like any web service would; see its own privacy policy. Direct video/GIF links are downloaded from the address you give.
- **Conversion.** Videos are converted to stickers on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Sticker library.** The stickers you make are saved in the app's private storage on your device so you can reuse them. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **WhatsApp.** When you tap "Add to WhatsApp", the sticker pack is handed to WhatsApp on your device through WhatsApp's official sticker-pack mechanism. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from there.
- **Pasteboard.** The app only reads the pasteboard when you tap the Paste button.
